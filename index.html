      Regulatory reference

      Labor Advisory No. 08-23

      DOLE issued Labor Advisory No. 08, series of 2023, “Safety and Health Measures to Prevent and Control Heat Stress at the Workplace,” pursuant to Article 168 of the Labor Code. It guides private-sector employers and workers, especially people working outdoors in extreme heat.

      Public accounts of the advisory direct employers to cut heat and humidity with ventilation and heat insulation, adjust rest breaks or the place of work, allow temperature-appropriate uniforms and UV-protective gear, provide free drinking water (about 2 to 3 liters a day), agree flexible hours that keep the same total work time, and keep a way to handle heat emergencies. DOLE regional offices and the Occupational Safety and Health Center’s regional extension units give technical help.

      The signed advisory is the controlling text. This page is an operational aid, not a reprint of it.

      Bands used here are the PAGASA effect-based classes: below 27.0°C not in a heat band; 27.0–32.9°C Caution; 33.0–41.9°C Extreme Caution; 42.0–51.9°C Danger; 52.0°C and above Extreme Danger. The meteorological index is the chart value. The +15°F solar load is an exposure adjustment from NWS and OSHA-NIOSH heat-safety practice. It is not part of the PAGASA forecast equation, and it can put an unshaded job in a higher band than the shade reading.
